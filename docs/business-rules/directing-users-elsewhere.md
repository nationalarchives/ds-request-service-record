# Directing users to another service

There are several circumstances when we tell users that we cannot provide, do not hold, or may not be
able to locate the record they want. These depend on whether the service person is alive, their service
branch, or the combination of service branch and commissioned officer status.

The guidance may direct users to another service, recommend waiting for records to be transferred, or
explain limitations on our ability to search. Not every warning prevents the user from continuing a request
if they choose to


## Current mappings

The conditions and messages below reflect the current journey.

| Condition | Message or page shown |
|-----------|-----------------------|
| Service person is alive | Subject Access Request |
| Royal Navy (including Royal Marines) | We do not hold this record |
| Home Guard | We are unlikely to be able to locate this record |
| British Army and commissioned officer | We are unlikely to hold this record yet (with Army specific guidance) |
| Royal Air Force and commissioned officer | We are unlikely to hold this record yet (with RAF specific guidance) |
| Other service branch and commissioned officer | We are unlikely to hold this record yet (with generic guidance) |
| Unknown service branch and commissioned officer | We are unlikely to hold this record yet (with generic guidance) |
| Born after 1939 | We do not have records for people born after 1939 |

The birth-year check is described in [Birth years covered](birth-years-covered.md).
